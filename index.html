Find dy/dx if y = x² sin x.

\(\dfrac{dy}{dx} = 2x\sin x + x^2\cos x\)
Steps (3)
  1. Product rule: \((uv)' = u'v + uv'\)
  2. \(u = x^2,\ u' = 2x\) and \(v = \sin x,\ v' = \cos x\)
  3. \(y' = 2x\sin x + x^2\cos x\)
